# Code of Conduct

This community is for people who love chess and want to get better at it. To keep it welcoming:

- **Be respectful.** No harassment, insults, hate speech or discrimination. Critique ideas, not people.
- **Stay on topic.** Slate Chess, chess improvement and chess in general are welcome; spam and promotion are not.
- **Play fair.** Don't ask for or share ways to cheat in rated games or competitions.
- **Keep it private.** Don't post other people's personal information, or your own payment or account details.
- **Be patient.** This is run by a small team.

We may edit, hide or remove posts, and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem by emailing support@slatechess.com or using GitHub's "Report content" menu.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
