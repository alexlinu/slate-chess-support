# Slate Chess Support & Community

Welcome. This is the home for help, questions, ideas and bug reports for **Slate Chess**, the chess coach for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, powered by Slate, our own chess engine.

Website: [slatechess.com](https://slatechess.com)

## Before you post

1. Search existing discussions and issues; your question may be answered.
2. Include your device, OS version and Slate Chess version (Settings › About).
3. Never post personal information, payment details or anything private. Games you share are public.
4. Be kind: see the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Beta testing

Early builds go out through TestFlight. Sign up at [slatechess.com](https://slatechess.com) and tell us what you want to test.
