# Frequently asked questions

## About Slate Chess

**What is Slate Chess?**
A chess coach for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro. It reviews your games and explains mistakes in plain language, plays opponents at your level, and helps you train what costs you points.

**What is Slate?**
Slate is the chess engine inside the app, written from scratch in Swift. Slate Chess is the app; Slate is the engine.

**Is it available yet?**
Not yet. Follow the [Announcements](https://github.com/alexlinu/slate-chess-support/discussions/categories/announcements) for news, and sign up for the beta at [slatechess.com](https://slatechess.com).

**How much does it cost?**
Pricing hasn't been announced.

## Privacy

**Are my games uploaded?**
No. Game analysis runs on your device, and no account is needed for core features. See the [Privacy Policy](https://slatechess.com/legal/privacy.html).

**Does the app collect any data?**
It may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app. You can turn this off in settings.

## Using the app

**How strong is Slate?**
About 3,150 on an engine rating scale. That is our own unofficial measurement (CCRL 40/15 scale, October 2026), and engine ratings aren't comparable with human ratings. [How we measure](https://slatechess.com/#strength).

**How accurate are the opponent levels?**
Levels from about 400 to grandmaster are approximate and calibrated against models of human play.

**Can I use Slate to cheat in rated games?**
No. Please don't use Slate Chess to get outside help in rated games, tournaments or competitions on any platform.

## Beta and billing

**How do I join the beta?**
Sign up at [slatechess.com](https://slatechess.com). Builds are shared through TestFlight as they're ready.

**Billing, refunds and subscriptions?**
Apple handles these. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings, and request refunds at https://support.apple.com/billing.
